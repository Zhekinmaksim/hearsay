A verdict nobody can recompute is a verdict nobody can contest. Take a row, break it, and watch the check catch it.

  

  

    

      
      
Three things are checked, and each catches a different lie. The envelope hash catches a row describing a different entry. The snapshot hash catches a verdict re-attributed to another page. And the verdict is recomputed from the votes as they were cast — not from itself — which catches a row whose votes do not add up to the verdict printed beside them.
